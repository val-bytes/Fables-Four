How to Play

Objective

Score points by filling your 4 field slots. The first player to reach the target score wins!

Turn Structure

  • Play one card from your hand into an empty field slot.
  • If you have no valid moves, click the deck to Discard a card and Draw a new one. This consumes your turn.

Scoring

  • Base Score: +1 Point for every unique number on your field.
  • Bonus Points: Triggered by matching adjacent values or matching set symbols.
